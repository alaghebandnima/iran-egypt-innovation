Disposable-income Gini is taken from SWIID 9.92 (April 2026), variable gini_disp, scale 0–100. Both countries have published values for every year in 2005–2023; only 2024 is filled by the project. Those 38 source values are standardized researcher estimates, not 38 independent annual household surveys. They are not spliced to World Bank consumption Ginis. SWIID standard errors are retained in the source data; validation against SWIID does not measure its own survey or harmonization uncertainty.

Sources: SWIID and Solt (2020), Social Science Quarterly 101(3):1183–1199; World Bank high-technology exports; World Bank / UNESCO R&D expenditure. Source URLs and formulas for all indicators are in the data dictionary. Historical events provide context, not proof of causation.
